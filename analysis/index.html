69 Games, 4,478 Shots: Our AI Pickleball Match Analysis

DATA · PBVISION · JUNE–NOVEMBER 2026 · DAVE & LOUISE ROBERTS

We film every match and run it through PBVision's AI. This is what the shot-by-shot data from 69 recorded games, at 12 venues in six countries, says about our game: serves through fifth shots, plus how often we actually get to the kitchen.

What the numbers say

The fourth shot got better

Our 160-game story found we were leaking points on shot four. So we drilled it. Comparing our first 34 games with the last 35, Dave's fourth shots went from 81% to 89% in, and from 63% to 74% unattackable. Louise's went from 80% to 88.5% in. It's the clearest improvement anywhere in the data.

Dave's third shot is now the weak link

With shot four fixed, the third shot stands out. It's Dave's least reliable shot (80% in) and the one where we win the fewest rallies (41%). It also separates wins from losses: in games we won, 82% of his thirds went in, against 77% in games we lost. He's mixing in more drives (38% of third shots early on, 45% lately), and the share of thirds that are unattackable has slipped slightly, from 59% to 56%.

Louise's drop is working

The first analysis suggested Louise should default to a drop on the third shot. Since then, drops have risen from 51% to 61% of her thirds, and her unattackable rate climbed from 45% to 56%, with 90% now landing in.

The fifth shot is Louise's next project

Louise's fifth shot is the one trending the wrong way: down from 80% in to 67.5%, with a rally win rate of just 32%. Third and fourth shots set the point up; the fifth is where it's currently being handed back.

Serves are close to automatic

Both of us land over 96% of serves. Dave hits slightly harder (32.8 mph on average against 31.5), but Louise serves deeper: 64% land deep, against 44% for Dave.

We don't get to the kitchen enough when serving

When receiving, we reach the kitchen line in 85–87% of rallies. When serving, it's less than half the time (49% for Dave, 46% for Louise). The serving team has further to travel, but it matters: we win more rallies when we get there. When Louise receives, we win 52% of rallies if she reaches the kitchen and only 37% if she doesn't.

How we collect it

A fixed sideline camera records every point of every match: no editing and no cherry-picked rallies. The footage goes through PBVision, which tags each shot by type, speed, quality and outcome.

Only Dave's and Louise's shots are counted here. Partners' and opponents' shots are left out, and a few games PBVision only partly captured are included as they are. Each city guide also links to the full PBVision breakdown of a match we played there.
