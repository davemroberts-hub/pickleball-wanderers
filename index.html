The footage does the arguing

Two parts to every stop: what we shoot, and what the AI finds in it afterward.

On camera, every point

A fixed sideline camera runs for the full match — no editing mid-game, no cherry-picked rallies. It's the same setup whether we're on a rooftop court in Bangkok or a converted tennis court in Portugal.

What 29 games say

One match tells you how you played. Twenty-nine tell you what's actually going wrong. Filter the shot-level data from every recorded game — Dave's played with Louise, Kim, Leah, and Dave P. along the way — by player, game, date, shot type, even which shot number in the rally.

Breakdown by shot type

How to read it: success% is the rate a shot landed in play without a fault; win% is the rate the rally was won when that shot occurred.
