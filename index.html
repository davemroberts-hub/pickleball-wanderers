Dave & Louise

We're two 50-something pickleball players travelling the world, chasing better games, better courts, and better versions of our own game.

We chase the best pickleball courts, wherever they are.

Two paddles, one carry-on, and a habit of finding the best pickleball in every city we land in. We play, we film every match, and we run it through AI to see what the footage won't tell you at first glance.

Pickleball City Guides

Real courts, real details, from the stops we know best. More get added as we go.

Seattle

USA

Sideout Tsunami is genuinely one of the best indoor venues in North America — 26 courts, gated 4.2+ sessions, six nights well spent.

Vancouver

CANADA

A decent destination, not Canada's best — but Ipop in Burnaby is a fantastic 9-court indoor facility right by a metro stop.

Edmonton

CANADA

Surprisingly good, with options at every level — three weeks here covered free outdoor courts, indoor open play, and coaching from Ava Weeks.

Montreal

CANADA

Pickle Montréal was our base — an indoor facility that's easy to book into as a visitor, and solid year-round.

Burlington

USA

Vermont's first dedicated indoor facility, Vermont Pickleball at The EDGE, is well built but membership-only — Catamount and the city parks are the easier drop-in options.

Puerto Vallarta

MEXICO

Well-run 3.5–4.0 open play at Parque Parota for around $3 — average courts, great value. Played 2023, rechecked for 2026.

Da Nang

VIETNAM

One of the best surprises on the route — G4 Pickleball delivered a strong 3.5–4.0 game with none of the membership friction found at the bigger resort clubs.

Bangkok

THAILAND

Drop-in play at Asoke Sports Club for ฿150 for two hours, with free public courts at Benchakitti a short walk away — easy access, average courts.

Hua Hin

THAILAND

T Rex hosted the 2026 Thailand Open — competitive open and graded play at 150 THB a session, let down only by badminton-surface courts.

Antalya

TURKEY

Five beachside courts at Ali Bey Resort Sorgun, open to resort guests only — a week of coaching with England team coaches and a strong standard of games.

Negombo

SRI LANKA

Pipinya is the only place to play — two floodlit outdoor courts at LKR 4,000 an hour, with beginner-friendly games, plus morning drilling by arrangement.

Night sessions under lights at the Aguada Pickleball Arena, right on Sinquerim Beach — a scenic stop while the local competitive scene is still finding its feet.

Bengaluru

Go Rally on Old Airport Road is one of five locations the chain now runs across the city — an easy, well-run indoor booking in one of India's fastest-growing pickleball scenes.

Ace Alley Pickleball in Viman Nagar has playable courts, but getting onto them as a visitor took more patience than the game itself.
