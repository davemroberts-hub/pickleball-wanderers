    How the ratings work

    Each player starts at their DUPR converted to Elo (1000 + (DUPR − 3.5) × 200). Players without a DUPR start at the group average. In doubles the two partners' ratings are averaged into a team rating. Winners take points from losers; an upset or a big score margin moves more points. The first 5 games are provisional and move faster. Ratings are shown back on the DUPR scale, with the Elo number underneath.

    How teams are picked

    Within each court the four players are split into the two teams with the closest combined rating. Across rounds it avoids repeating partners and opponents, and anyone who sat out gets priority to play next. Mixed levels spreads players across courts; Similar levels puts the strongest players together on court 1, and so on down. Rounds are kept on this device only.
