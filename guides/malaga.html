Where to Play Pickleball in Málaga

Pickleball on the Costa del Sol is still young but growing fast — from dedicated pay-to-play courts out at Mijas to small members-only setups closer to the city centre. We played at Daryl Collins Tennis Academy in Mijas (listed as Pickleball Mijas Fuengirola), and analyzed the match with PBVision.

Daryl Collins Tennis Academy

Mijas · Outdoor · 8 courts

Listed as Pickleball Mijas Fuengirola, this is one of the bigger dedicated pickleball setups on the Costa del Sol — 8 outdoor acrylic courts with permanent lines and nets, pay-to-play with no membership required. The academy also has food and drinks, water, restrooms, locker rooms, lighting for evening play, and on-site coaching.

We played here as part of a Pickleball Pro Retreats trip, coached by Tom Corfield.

Our take: a proper dedicated facility rather than a repurposed tennis court, and easy for travelling players to drop into without needing to join anything.

Club Deportivo Pickleball Málaga

Málaga city · Outdoor · 4 courts

A members-only club closer to the city centre, with 4 outdoor hard courts, permanent lines, portable nets, and lights. Court reservations are available for members.

4 outdoor courts Members only Pl. de John F. Kennedy, Málaga

Pickleball Colegio La Colina

Málaga · Coached sessions

Run by Antonio, an Andalusian Tennis Federation pickleball instructor, who hosts "American-style" games with the local community — mini-games where winners move up a court and losers move down, so you end up playing people at your own level. Bookable as a class, a games session, or both.

Coach-led sessions Booked via Airbnb Experiences 29190, Málaga, Andalusia

Our Málaga Pickleball Tips

Head out to Mijas for the most courts

Daryl Collins Tennis Academy has the biggest dedicated footprint on the Costa del Sol and doesn't require membership.

Check access before making the trip into the city

Club Deportivo Pickleball Málaga is members-only, so confirm access before heading there as a visitor.

Bring your own paddle

Worth confirming demo equipment availability ahead of time, especially at the smaller venues.

The Wanderers Take

Málaga's pickleball scene is still small compared to the bigger European hubs, but Daryl Collins Tennis Academy gave us a real dedicated facility and a competitive game with no membership friction. Worth a stop if you're on the Costa del Sol.
