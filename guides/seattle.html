Seattle

USA

Six nights here, and we were lucky enough to land on one of the best indoor venues in North America.

Sideout Tsunami

Indoor · 26 courts

A fabulous 26-court indoor venue that's been open for a year. Open play sessions run every day for all levels, including gated 4.2+ DUPR sessions. We played the advanced 3.7+ session and the level was very good — genuinely one of the best indoor venues in the whole of North America.

26 indoor courts 6am – 11pm, daily Open play, all levels Gated 4.2+ DUPR sessions

Green Lake Park

Public outdoor · 8 courts

We were staying in the Green Lake area, so went to check out the local setup. 8 outdoor courts, run on a paddle-stack system. We visited in the evening and found it busy but beginner-friendly.
