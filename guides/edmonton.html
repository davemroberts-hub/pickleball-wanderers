Edmonton

CANADA

This is a surprisingly good pickleball destination with lots of great options for players at all levels. We were there for three weeks and managed to play at a number of different places.

Confederation Park

Free outdoor · 8 courts

Eight excellent new outdoor courts, free for public use. We had some good drilling sessions here, though the level of play during the daytime wasn't high. Floodlit and open all day, every day. Similar courts exist at other locations around the city.

8 outdoor courts Free Floodlit Open all day, every day

Pickleball Hub — Roper Road

Indoor · 8 courts

Pickleball Hub has 3 locations in and around the city — we played open play sessions at the Roper Road site. Fun sessions, though not the highest level of play.

8 indoor courts 3 city locations Open play sessions
pickleballhub.playbypoint.com →

Edmonton Sports Centre

7 dedicated courts

We managed a few private sessions here — 7 dedicated courts, open from 6am to 11pm every day. We also had some excellent coaching from Canadian pro Ava Weeks at this facility.

7 dedicated courts 6am – 11pm, daily Private coaching available
