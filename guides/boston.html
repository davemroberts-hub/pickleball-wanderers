Boston

USA

Our first stop on our latest pickleball travels through North America — we wanted to know where a travelling player can actually get good games, not just find courts on a map.

PickleBOS

Charlestown · Indoor · 10 courts

A great new indoor facility with 10 indoor courts, all equipped with Podplay cameras. The crowd here is friendly, the open play sessions are reasonably priced, and there are sessions for every level, including a dedicated 3.5–4.0 group.

10 indoor courts 7am – 10pm, daily $20 open play $110/hour private court

The booking process using the Podplay app was straightforward, and the host met us when we arrived to point us to our courts. The session was a pure open-play "dink and drink," so we didn't expect much — but there were genuinely good players there, and people self-selected into fairly evenly matched groups. Dave got into some solid 4.0-level games. His agility and return game were the standouts; serve consistency was the area PBVision flagged to work on.

We'd definitely recommend a session at PickleBOS for any players visiting Boston. The courts are great and there are lots of options for players at all levels. However, it's worth noting that private court rental is very expensive.

We didn't play at these venues ourselves this trip, but they're worth knowing about if PickleBOS in Charlestown doesn't fit your schedule or location.
