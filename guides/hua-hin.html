Hua Hin

Thailand

Where to Play Pickleball in Hua Hin

Hua Hin is a stop we've come back to. We played open play and graded play at T Rex in both 2025 and 2026, and in January 2026 we played the Thailand Open there. We weren't interested in simply finding courts on a map — we wanted to know where a travelling player can actually get good games, and at what level.

Guide checked: October 2026 · Last played: January 2026

T Rex — Hua Hin

Hua Hin · 8 courts

T Rex has 8 courts and was the venue for the Thailand Open in January 2026. It runs both open play and graded play, so you can choose sessions matched to your level rather than taking pot luck. The games here are competitive and well matched.

Sign-up is through the Stack Team app. You'll need a code to join, which you can get from the T Rex Facebook group. Sessions are 150 THB (about $5) — among the best value on our route, alongside Da Nang.

Our take: strong, well-graded games at a very low price. The courts themselves are the weak point — they have a badminton surface rather than a dedicated pickleball surface.

8 courts (badminton surface) Sessions 150 THB (~$5) Open play & graded play Book via Stack Team app

Our Hua Hin Pickleball Tips

Get your Stack Team code first

T Rex sessions are booked through the Stack Team app, and you need a code to join. Grab it from the T Rex Facebook group before you try to sign up.

Use graded play to find your level

Alongside open play, T Rex runs graded sessions — the quickest way for a visitor to get into well-matched games.

Check the tournament calendar

T Rex hosted the Thailand Open in January 2026. If you're visiting around tournament time, it's worth seeing whether you can enter.

Budget is not an issue

At 150 THB (about $5) a session, you can play every day without thinking about cost.

The Wanderers Take

Hua Hin earns its place on our route on the strength of the games, not the courts. T Rex delivers competitive open play and graded play at 150 THB a session, and hosted the Thailand Open in January 2026. The badminton-surface courts are the one real drawback. If you're planning a wider trip through Asia, our Da Nang and Goa guides are good stops to compare against.
