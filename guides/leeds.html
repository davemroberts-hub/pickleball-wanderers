Where to Play Pickleball in Leeds

Leeds is where it all started for us — Game Set Match, playing out of John Charles Centre for Sport, is our home club and the place we learnt to play pickleball. It's the standard every court on this route gets measured against.

Game Set Match — John Charles Centre for Sport

Belle Isle · Indoor · 8 courts

Game Set Match runs Yorkshire's biggest pickleball setup on 8 indoor acrylic courts at John Charles Centre for Sport, south Leeds. Sessions run all levels together — genuinely all levels, from complete beginners through to players rated 4.5+, which makes it a good stop whatever stage you're at.

Pickleball runs four days a week: Monday (12:00–14:00 Social, 18:00–19:30 Beginners, 19:00–21:00 Intermediate/Advanced), Wednesday (same as Monday minus the lunchtime slot — 18:00–19:30 Beginners, 19:00–21:00 Intermediate/Advanced), and lunchtime-only sessions on Thursday and Friday (12:00–14:00). Sessions are £12.50.

Our take: this is where our whole pickleball journey started, and it's still our benchmark for what a well-run club session looks like — genuinely mixed levels, proper coaching pedigree behind it, and a strong competitive edge when you want one.

Middleton Leisure Centre

Middleton · Indoor · 3 courts

A smaller, pay-to-play indoor option on the south side of the city — three wood courts, run through Active Leeds. Worth knowing about if GSM's Monday sessions don't line up with your schedule.

Our Leeds Pickleball Tips

Four days a week, not just one

Sessions run Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday — Monday and Wednesday have the fullest schedule (social plus beginners and intermediate/advanced), Thursday and Friday are lunchtime-only.

All levels genuinely means all levels

Don't be put off if you're new — sessions mix beginners with 4.5+ players, and it works.

New to the game? Start Monday or Wednesday evening

The 18:00–19:30 Beginners slot on Monday and Wednesday is the easiest way in if you're just starting out.
