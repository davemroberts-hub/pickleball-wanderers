Pickleball Travel Guides

We've played pickleball in cities across Europe, Asia and North America. These are our first-hand guides for travelling players.

Europe

Leeds

UK

Home club — Game Set Match at John Charles Centre for Sport is where we learnt to play, and still where we measure everywhere else against.

Read the guide →

Sheffield

UK

Peak Pickleball in Attercliffe is a small, friendly club run by two local enthusiasts — easy to drop into, with games for every level from beginner to 4.0+.

Wrexham

WALES

A regular UK stop for us — Wrexham Tennis & Padel Centre has grown to 8 courts since pickleball landed here in 2025, with great value and coaching on tap.

Read the guide →

Hampshire

UK

Three setups across the county — Drills & Play at Hurlands in Farnham, £12 advanced open play at Dinks, and a PickleB tournament at the Hampshire Court Hotel.

Read the guide →

Málaga

SPAIN

Played a Pickleball Pro Retreats session at Daryl Collins Tennis Academy in Mijas, coached by Tom Corfield — 8 dedicated courts and a genuinely competitive game.

Rota

SPAIN

PORTUGAL

Played as part of a touring group session with Leigh Pickleball — a sign of how active the touring scene is on this coast.

North America

Seattle

USA

Sideout Tsunami is genuinely one of the best indoor venues in North America — 26 courts, gated 4.2+ sessions, six nights well spent.

Vancouver

CANADA

A decent destination, not Canada's best — but Ipop in Burnaby is a fantastic 9-court indoor facility right by a metro stop.

CANADA

Surprisingly good, with options at every level — three weeks here covered free outdoor courts, indoor open play, and coaching from Ava Weeks.

CANADA

Pickle Montréal was our base — an indoor facility that's easy to book into as a visitor, and solid year-round.
