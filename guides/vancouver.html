Where to Play Pickleball in Vancouver

This is a decent pickleball destination, but definitely not the best in Canada.

Guide checked: September 2026 · Last played: August 2026

Ipop, Burnaby

Indoor · 9 courts

A fantastic indoor facility with 9 courts. It's easily accessible without a car, right next to a metro stop. Regular open play sessions for all levels, plus clinics and private coaching. IPOP was founded by Chris White, who — fittingly — goes by "chief paddle officer."

Would we go back? To Ipop, yes without hesitation — it's the clear standout in the city, easy to book into as a visitor, and well served by transit. Vancouver overall is a solid stop rather than a must-visit one; if Ipop weren't here, the city wouldn't rank as highly on its own.

The Wanderers Take

Vancouver is a decent stop, not a standout one — and almost all of that comes down to Ipop. It's a genuinely excellent facility: nine courts, easy metro access, and open play for every level, which is why our overall Vancouver rating leans on it so heavily. The public options are fine as a backup, but neither Chinatown nor Queen Elizabeth Park gave us a reason to rank the city higher on their own. If you're mapping out a wider North America trip, our Seattle, Edmonton and Boston guides are worth comparing against.
